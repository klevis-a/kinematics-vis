Axial Orientation Trajectory View

This is a custom view for showcasing axial orientation trajectory concepts.

Features:

  • Humerus bone with attached triad showing orientation
  • Globe (wireframe sphere) for spatial reference
  • Angular velocity vector visualization
  • Axial plane (forearm representation)
  • Four custom trajectories (Trial1-Trial4)

Controls:

  • Use the timeline slider to navigate through frames
  • Toggle trajectory visibility using the Trial buttons
  • Mouse controls: Left click + drag to rotate, Right click + drag to pan, Scroll to zoom
